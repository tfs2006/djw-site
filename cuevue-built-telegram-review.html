Quick FAQ

Why are you recommending this, David?

Honestly, this one's a conditional pick: if you already run CueVue Built, the Telegram layer is a genuinely useful unlock for prospecting on the move. I'm not recommending the underlying platform sight unseen — I haven't tested it — but the chat-interface concept solves a real friction I've felt with dashboard-bound tools.

Do I need CueVue Built first?

Yes — the sales page states the bot runs your Built Engine missions and needs an active Built account. Missions use the same allowances and credits as the web app.

Does it send emails to prospects automatically?

No. The page is explicit: missions prepare pitch pages and emails, and you review the facts and recipient, then send from Built Engine, your email, or by sharing the link. Nothing is sent without you.

How do I set it up?

Message @BotFather in Telegram, create a bot, copy the token, paste it into Built's Settings under Telegram, and tap Start on the link it shows you. About two minutes, per the vendor.

Can I stop a mission from Telegram?

Yes — tap Stop on the progress card or send /stop. Scheduled missions cancel the same way, and /disconnect unlinks the bot entirely.
