The AI Video Stack Has a Missing Middle — Stitching Is It

2026's AI video generators got stunningly good at 5-second clips and no better at finishing a video. That created a weird workflow: creators prompt, generate, regenerate, burn credits, and then manually assemble everything in CapCut or Premiere. The 'stitcher' category — AI-assisted timeline editors — exists to close that gap.

The honest question for any tool here is output quality per hour of your time. AI assembly saves editing labor, but the clips still need taste: pacing, music, narrative. Tools like this raise the floor of what one person can publish; they don't replace the judgment of what deserves to be published.
