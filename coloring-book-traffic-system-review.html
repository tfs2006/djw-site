The Real Reason Most Coloring Books Don't Sell: Nobody Sees Them

The low-content book boom flooded KDP and Etsy with coloring books — tens of thousands of publishers, many using AI art. When supply explodes, the scarce resource stops being the book and becomes attention. Amazon and Etsy rank listings by sales velocity and relevance, which creates a chicken-and-egg problem: no traffic means no sales, no sales means no rank, no rank means no traffic.

That's why organic traffic training for this niche makes sense on paper: Pinterest, TikTok, and YouTube are discovery engines where a single viral flip-through video can break the cycle. The honest caveat is that organic traffic is a skill and a grind — it compounds, but slowly, and anyone promising fast free traffic is selling the sizzle.
