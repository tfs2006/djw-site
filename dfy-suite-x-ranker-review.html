What 2020-Era Ranking Tools Got Right (and What's Changed)

The core insight of tools like X Ranker was correct and still is: video results get prominent placement in Google search, and ranking a video is often easier than ranking a web page for the same keyword. The keyword-research-plus-syndication playbook hasn't stopped working as a concept.

A ranking tool is only as good as its maintenance — APIs break, tactics decay. That's the entire risk with vintage software: the strategy is timeless, but the code may not be.
