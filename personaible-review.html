Why Personalized Video Beats Templated Outreach

The mechanism is sound and well-documented: a prospect who sees their own business name and website in the first seconds of a video keeps watching, where a templated email gets deleted. Directionally, personalization outperforming mass templates is one of the sturdier findings in outreach.

The honest caveat is volume and targeting. Personalized outreach converts better per-send but scales slower than mass email, and no video overcomes a weak offer or a bad list. Treat this as a format upgrade for an outreach process you already run — not a business in a box.
